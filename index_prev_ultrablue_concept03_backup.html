P.01

Alpha Rating is relative currency

Use it to compare players on expected pro outcome. Do not read it as a fantasy-points forecast.

↗
P.02

Freshman placements are projections

Incoming freshmen have no college production yet, so their position is not backtested.

↗
P.03

Position reliability is uneven

The board explicitly treats the evidence strength at WR, RB, QB and TE differently.

↗
P.04

Policy choices stay labeled

League-format priors and other stated assumptions should remain visually distinct from fitted or backtested evidence.

How to read this board

  • Alpha Rating is a relative currency, not a points projection. It ranks players against each other — it does not forecast fantasy points.
  • The TE level and freshman discount are stated league-format priors — policy choices, not numbers fitted or backtested against outcomes.
  • Freshmen (marked FR) have no college production yet. Their placement on the board is a projection and is not backtested.

Fantrax — college (CFF / devy) or NFL leagues
Open your league on fantrax.com and copy the address — it looks like fantrax.com/fantasy/league/abc123xyz/… For C2C, add both the college and the NFL league; teams with the same name become one franchise.
Sleeper — dynasty leagues, or the NFL side of a C2C

How this works

  • Built for three kinds of league. C2C: link your college and NFL sides — on Fantrax, or Fantrax college + Sleeper NFL — and run the whole franchise as one. Dynasty: connect a Sleeper or Fantrax NFL league on its own. College fantasy (CFF): add a Fantrax college league on its own.
  • One page, every league. The Viewing switcher picks a Fantrax league set, any Sleeper league, or a linked C2C. Start / Sit and projection-based waivers work for Sleeper leagues (Fantrax doesn't publish projections). All Leagues checks every Sleeper lineup at once and shows the players you hold in more than one league.
  • Read-only, no login. Fantrax needs just the league ID; Sleeper just your username. Neither app lets outside tools submit trades or claims, so every offer has a copy button — paste it in yourself.
  • One scale, both sides. College players are valued on the live Alpha Universe devy board; NFL players on the AU NFL dynasty values. Both sit on the same scale, so a college prospect and a pro can be weighed in one deal — but read a close college-vs-pro verdict as a tier comparison, not point-for-point.
  • C2C franchises. Add your college league and your NFL league; teams with matching names are combined into one franchise, so rankings and trades see both rosters. If your NFL side is on Sleeper, link it below the leagues — each Sleeper manager is matched to his Fantrax team (exact name, the name in parentheses, or by elimination; you can fix any pairing), and trade offers can then mix college and NFL players across both apps.
  • Long-term value, not weekly points. AU values rank players on their expected pro outcome. In season-long college fantasy leagues, use them for stashes, keepers and trades — not to set this week's lineup.
  • Freshmen (FR) have no college production yet — their value is a projection, not backtested. Players off the AU board carry no value and aren't used in offers.
  • Fair offers land within the value band you set. Two-for-ones are only offered when both pieces are real (no throw-ins under a quarter of the value) and neither beats the single player coming back — a raw sum flatters the side with more bodies.

How this works

  • Read-only. The tool reads Sleeper's public API with just your username — no password, no login. Sleeper doesn't let outside tools submit lineups, claims or trades, so every recommendation is something you tap in the Sleeper app yourself. Trade offers come with a copy button so you can paste them fast.
  • Start/sit uses your league's scoring. Weekly projections are re-scored with each league's own scoring settings (6-pt pass TDs, TE premium, PPR — whatever the league uses), then the best legal lineup is built for your roster slots, flex and superflex included.
  • Trade and waiver values use the DevyDose NFL dynasty values (superflex scale) in dynasty and keeper leagues. In redraft leagues they switch to the next three weeks of projected points. Rookie picks and players beyond the value list carry no value here, so the trade finder won't offer them.
  • Mass trades: pick one of your players to shop and the tool finds the best fair return from every other team. Or pick a target and it builds offers from your roster. Or let it scan the league for swaps that fill a need on both sides. A fair offer lands within the value band you set.
  • Projections are a guide, not a guarantee. Check late injury news before kickoff.
